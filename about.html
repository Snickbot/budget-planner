About This Site

Monthly Budget Planner is a free, simple tool built to help everyday people track their income and expenses without needing to sign up, install an app, or hand over personal data.

The idea came from a common problem: most budgeting apps are either too complicated, require a login, or push constant notifications. This tool does one thing well — it lets you enter your monthly income, log your expenses by category, and instantly see whether you're staying within budget.

All your data stays on your own device. Nothing is sent to a server, and nothing is shared with anyone. You can use it privately, for free, for as long as you like.

This site is independently built and maintained. It's a work in progress, and new features (like savings goals and monthly comparisons) are planned for the future.
